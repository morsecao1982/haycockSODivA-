🔬 Division A Events

2026–2027 Season · 22 events across 3rd Grade and Upper Division

Division A Science Olympiad events are organized into two tiers. 3rd Grade events are designed for younger students; Upper Division events are open to all eligible Division A participants. Click any event name to view full details on the Virginia Science Olympiad website.

3rd Grade Events
🎈

Balloon Racer

3rd Grade · Engineering

Teams construct and test an air-powered balloon device that travels along a fishing line track, minimizing time. Participants also answer questions on Newton's Laws and air pressure.

🎨

Picture This

3rd Grade · Inquiry

Team members take turns drawing scientific terms while teammates identify each term based on visual representations, covering all major science disciplines.

Official Page ↗
🌿

Biomes

3rd Grade · Life Science

Participants are assessed on their understanding of ecological principles, ecosystems, species interactions, and major terrestrial and aquatic biomes.

Official Page ↗
🌉

Truss Bridge

3rd Grade · Engineering

Teams design and construct a truss bridge spanning a 30 cm gap, then test it by supporting masses up to 500 grams, with a written assessment on structural design.

Official Page ↗
🫀

Human Body

3rd Grade · Life Science

Participants are assessed on their understanding of the structure, function, and organization of major human body systems and the organs that comprise them.

Upper Division Events
🚀

Astronaut Lander

Upper Division · Engineering

Teams design and construct a landing device that safely protects a simulated astronaut from a designated height, including a simulated purchasing activity and written assessment on reentry physics.

Official Page ↗
🪂

Bungee Drop

Upper Division · Engineering

Teams design and construct a bungee cord system to drop a weight from a designated height, plus a written assessment on energy conservation and elastic potential energy.

Official Page ↗
🔐

Codebusters

Upper Division · Inquiry

Teams use cryptanalysis techniques to decrypt encrypted messages using a variety of historical and modern cipher systems.

Energy

Genetics

Glider

Teams build and test one glider of any design using a cereal box they bring to the competition, along with materials provided on site.

Hovercraft

Upper Division · Engineering

Teams design, construct, and test a balloon hovercraft that levitates on a cushion of air, competing to stop it as close as possible to a finish line, plus a written assessment on physics.

Hydraulics

Upper Division · Engineering

Teams build a hydraulic system to raise a 100-gram weighted object as high as possible while maintaining the lift, and complete a written assessment on hydraulic principles.

Optics
