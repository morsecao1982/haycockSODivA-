About Division A

Science Olympiad Division A is an elementary school science competition program for students in grades 3–5. Teams of students compete in a variety of science, engineering, and hands-on events — covering topics from biology and earth science to physics and technology. Haycock Elementary is proud to bring this program to our students for the 2026–2027 school year.
